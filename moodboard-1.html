TRANSLATION INTO LEON

Jewelry appears as a strange heirloom: close to skin, partly concealed, discovered through reflection. Use asymmetric framing, soft focus against one razor-sharp jewel, and restrained calligraphic gestures.

Avoid gothic stock symbols, crowns, skulls and decorative flourishes without a reason.

Curated reference study • Original imagery retains its original colors. Black, aubergine and purple define the proposed LEON treatment. Existing brand marks are references; font specimens are exploratory typesetting. Photography and marks remain credited to their respective creators.
